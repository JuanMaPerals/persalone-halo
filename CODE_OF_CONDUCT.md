# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive and evidence-led.
- Welcome different viewpoints and correct mistakes gracefully.
- Protect privacy and do not pressure anyone to share personal, voice or device data.
- Keep safety claims proportionate to the evidence available.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language, intimidation, sexualised content or attention, deliberate disruption, doxxing, publishing private information, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate are not acceptable. This includes sharing credentials, private recordings, transcripts or device identifiers without clear permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainers through the non-public contact route associated with the repository. When GitHub is configured, do not use a public issue for sensitive reports. Maintainers may correct behaviour, issue a warning, remove content, temporarily restrict participation or permanently ban a participant, proportionate to the impact and pattern of conduct.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone represents the project in public spaces. Retaliation against a reporter is not acceptable.
